# Reflection

This scenario showed the importance of recognizing patterns across multiple user reports and grouping related tickets into one main incident.

The investigation should be driven by evidence rather than assumptions. Different findings can lead to different actions: Content Ops for asset issues, DevOps/Infra for system or CDN issues, or user-side troubleshooting for browser/network problems.

Clear ticket grouping, documentation, and synchronized communication help avoid duplicated investigation and keep all affected users informed consistently.
