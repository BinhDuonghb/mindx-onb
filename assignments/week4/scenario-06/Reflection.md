# Classification Justification

Class of Service: Fixed Deadline

Reason:
The request has a hard business deadline at 09:00 and the main priority is delivering the required report within the agreed time.
The classification is driven by the deadline rather than the number of affected users.

# Reflection

This scenario showed the importance of clarifying scope before committing to a fixed deadline.

When time is limited, Support should separate the minimum required deliverable from nice-to-have items, confirm data access and privacy requirements, and raise risks early instead of promising work that may not be achievable.

Clear scope confirmation, approval, and regular timeline updates help protect both the deadline and stakeholder expectations.
