Method, not mood.

- Why learn

- How to learn with discipline

---

## Why Learn?

<img src="https://mindx.edu.vn/logo.svg" alt="MindX Logo" class="mindx-logo" />

- To grow into your next role
- To solve better problems at work
- To become useful, not passive
- To avoid wasting free time

**Do not learn randomly.**

Learn because it supports your career path.

---

## What to Learn?

<img src="https://mindx.edu.vn/logo.svg" alt="MindX Logo" class="mindx-logo" />

Example for a Software Engineer:

```
Now:      Junior Software Engineer
Target:   Mid-level Software Engineer
Gap:      System design, testing, ownership
```

Ask:

- What does the next level expect?
- Which skills are missing today?
- Which 1 skill should I focus on first?

**Do not learn based on trends or mood.**

---

## When and Where?

<img src="https://mindx.edu.vn/logo.svg" alt="MindX Logo" class="mindx-logo" />

Learn inside your real work environment:

- Free time at work -> learn, do not stay idle
- Learn something small -> apply it immediately
- Use real problems as learning material

Example rhythm:

- Daily: 30 minutes
- Weekly: reflect and report back
- Monthly: check progress

---

## Who is Responsible?

<img src="https://mindx.edu.vn/logo.svg" alt="MindX Logo" class="mindx-logo" />

Three roles:

1. **You** -> own your learning
2. **Mentor** -> review and guide
3. **Peers** -> share and keep accountability

**Your career is your responsibility.**

---

## How to Learn?

<img src="https://mindx.edu.vn/logo.svg" alt="MindX Logo" class="mindx-logo" />

Use a simple method:

1. Pick **one skill** for a period of time
2. Use **macro learning** to build depth
3. Use **micro learning** in small daily steps
4. Apply it in real work
5. Report what you learned

Example:

- **Macro learning** -> a course, a book, or guided practice
- **Micro learning** -> 10-15 minute notes, videos, docs, small exercises

**Depth + repetition + application = real growth**

---

## Discipline Over Mood

<img src="https://mindx.edu.vn/logo.svg" alt="MindX Logo" class="mindx-logo" />

- Do not wait until you feel inspired
- Do not study only what feels interesting
- Do not sit idle when work is quiet

Instead:

- Learn on schedule
- Learn for a reason
- Learn, apply, and report back

**Discipline builds careers.**
